# Cold Email Sequence: Partnership Outreach

## When to Use
For reaching out to potential partners who could refer clients:
- Agencies serving your target market
- Complementary product companies
- Consultants in your space
- Influencers/creators in your niche

---

### Email 1 (Day 0) — The Win-Win
**Subject:** {their industry} + {your solution type}

**Body:**
Hey {FirstName},

I run {your company} — {one sentence about what you do}.

I'm looking for {2-3} {partner type} to partner with. The deal:

- You refer clients who need {what you solve}
- I handle all {delivery/tech/service}
- You earn {X}% of monthly revenue for the life of the client

Your clients get {outcome}. You get recurring revenue on a service you don't deliver. I get distribution.

Worth a 15-min call to see if this fits?

{YourName}

---

### Email 2 (Day 5) — Show the Product
**Subject:** re: {same subject}

**Body:**
Hey {FirstName},

Following up with a quick demo of what the product looks like in practice: {Loom/demo link}

{2-3 sentences describing what they'll see in the demo.}

If any of your clients are dealing with {problem}, happy to do a joint demo.

{YourName}

---

### Email 3 (Day 10) — Numbers + Breakup
**Subject:** re: {same subject}

**Body:**
Hey {FirstName},

Last follow-up. A few data points:

{Bullet 1: key metric/result}
{Bullet 2: key metric/result}
{Bullet 3: key metric/result}

If {problem area} isn't something your clients need help with right now, no worries. But if it comes up, I'm here: {Calendly link}

{YourName}

---

## Revenue Share Math Template
- Client pays: ${X}/month
- Partner gets: {Y}% = ${Z}/month recurring
- Per year per client: ${Z × 12}
- Refer 5 clients: ${Z × 12 × 5}/year in passive revenue
