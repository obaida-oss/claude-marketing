# Cold Email Sequence: High-Intent Outreach

## When to Use
For prospects showing buying signals:
- Left negative reviews on competitor products
- Posted about the problem on social media or forums
- Actively searching for solutions (job postings, forum questions)

---

### Email 1 (Day 0) — Reference Their Signal
**Subject:** {reference to their public signal, e.g., "your [competitor] review"}

**Body:**
Hey {FirstName},

{Reference their specific public signal — their review, tweet, forum post, etc.}

{Acknowledge their frustration — paraphrase their own words back to them.}

{One sentence about how you solve that specific problem differently.}

{Low-friction CTA.}

{YourName}

---

### Email 2 (Day 4) — The Contrast
**Subject:** re: {same subject}

**Body:**
Hey {FirstName},

Quick follow-up. Here's the difference between {current approach} and what I built:

{Current approach}: {specific limitation they complained about}
{Your approach}: {specific way you solve it}

{Link to demo.}

{YourName}

---

## Where to Find High-Intent Signals
- App store/product reviews (1-3 stars on competitors)
- Reddit/forum complaints
- Twitter threads about the problem
- Job postings (hiring for roles your product replaces)
- G2/Capterra reviews
