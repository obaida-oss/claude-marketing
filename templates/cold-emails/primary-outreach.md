# Cold Email Sequence: Primary Outreach

## Before Using
- Run `/setup-context` to set up your product marketing context
- Run `/cold-emails` to generate sequences tailored to YOUR product
- These are structural templates — customize with your specifics

---

### Email 1 (Day 0) — Observation + Value
**Subject:** {2-4 words, lowercase, related to their world}

**Body:**
Hey {FirstName},

{Specific observation about their company — something you noticed that connects to the problem you solve.}

{One sentence about the problem — framed from THEIR perspective, not yours.}

{One sentence about what you built/do — specific, not generic.}

{One proof point — a number, a result, a case study snippet.}

{Low-friction CTA — "Worth exploring?" / "Want a 3-min video walkthrough?"}

{YourName}

---

### Email 2 (Day 3) — Proof + Demo
**Subject:** re: {same subject}

**Body:**
Hey {FirstName},

Quick follow-up — {share a specific example, case study, or before/after}.

{Show the demo or proof in 2-3 lines.}

{Link to demo video or resource.}

{YourName}

---

### Email 3 (Day 7) — Breakup
**Subject:** re: {same subject}

**Body:**
Hey {FirstName},

Last note — if {problem} isn't a priority right now, no worries.

But if you're curious: {link to demo/resource}.

Either way, good luck with {CompanyName}.

{YourName}

---

## Personalization Research Checklist
- [ ] What does their company do? (check website)
- [ ] What tools do they use? (check job postings, tech stack tools)
- [ ] Any recent news? (funding, hiring, product launch)
- [ ] Any public complaints about the problem you solve? (reviews, tweets, forum posts)

## Key Principles (from cold-email skill)
- Write like a peer, not a vendor
- Every sentence must earn its place
- Lead with their world, not yours
- One ask, low friction
- No "I hope this email finds you well"
